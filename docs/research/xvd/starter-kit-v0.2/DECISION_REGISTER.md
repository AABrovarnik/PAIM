## Decision Advisor / CHALLENGE
Question, urgency, options incl. do nothing, hard gates, simplest adequate solution, review level L1/L2/L3 and reason, incremental value/cost, risk, confidence, owner decision and next trigger. Separate reviewer where required; no simulated independence. Do not convene a board for every minor question. Reopen when evidence or risk materially changes.
